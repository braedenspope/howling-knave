How Training Works

The crew of the Howling Knave will teach you what they know — but only if you put in the hours. Here is how the work pays off.

schedule

Your day is eight hours

Each day on the board is eight one-hour blocks (sleep is assumed). Some hours get claimed by ship duties — the work that keeps you afloat. Whatever is left is yours to spend training with the crew.

Earn Progress Points

Training is run hour by hour. Every hour of a session is its own roll, and every roll that lands is worth one Progress Point (PP) — so a longer session is worth more because you sit through more of it, not because it counts for extra.

A wasted day is never wasted

Miss every roll of a training on a given day and you still walk away with 1 PP for the attempt. A day at the mast is never worth nothing — you can have a terrible day, but you can't have an empty one.
