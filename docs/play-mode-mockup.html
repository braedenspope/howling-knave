The Montage Runner

A clickable stand-in for play mode — not the real app, but wired to the same rules. Step through a two-day voyage for three players and watch the points land. One roll per hour; every roll that lands is a point; a training whose rolls all miss on a day still earns one for the attempt.

The gate into the montage. Every day must be sealed by every player before the run can start, then the crew are put in the order they will take their hours.

All hands sealed

Turn order

Set once for the voyage. Everyone takes hour 1 in this order, then hour 2, and so on.

Progress so far

Not a screen in the app — a window into the scoring so you can check the rules as you click.

Fonts fall back to system serifs here; the real app loads Cinzel and IM Fell English. Crew colours, session lengths, PP thresholds and the turn-order loop are taken from the live curriculum.
